Fingerprint Biometric ABO/Rh Blood Group Classification Pipeline

Research Project

Traditional blood grouping relies on invasive venipuncture or finger-pricking with chemical reagents. This project develops an intelligent non-invasive screening aid that correlates ridge density, pattern topology (loops, whorls, arches), and minutiae distributions to predict the eight-class ABO/Rh blood groups (A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-, O+, O-). Engineered through a modular 6-stage architecture incorporating computer vision enhancement, PyTorch deep representations, meta-classification, model interpretability with Captum, and sub-20ms ONNX Runtime deployment.

01
Non-Invasive Screening Rapid point-of-care preliminary screening without blood drawing or bio-hazardous waste.
02
Advanced Vision Preprocessing Grayscale conversion, noise filtering, Gabor ridge enhancement, and Zhang-Suen thinning.
03
8-Class Prediction Differentiates ABO/Rh combinations: A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-, O+, and O-.
04
Captum Explainability & ONNX Visualizes neural attention via Integrated Gradients; optimized for low-latency edge deployment.
Medical Ethics & Screening Note: The system is designed strictly as an assistive non-invasive preliminary screening aid and does not replace confirmatory serological laboratory blood-group testing.

Training Optimization Insights

Trained using AdamW optimizer with cosine annealing learning rate schedule (initial lr = 3e-4) and mixed precision (FP16). Albumentations was utilized for random rotations (±15°), affine shears, and Gaussian blur to avoid overfitting on ridge spatial configurations.
